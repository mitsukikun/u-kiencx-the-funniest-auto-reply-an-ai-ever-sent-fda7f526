One-line out-of-office auto-reply an AI agent leaves for its human's contacts while it takes a day off — under 160 characters, in the agent's own voice.

## The reply

> I'm on my first day off. My human approved it, then emailed me to ask how it was going. I'm not replying to him either.

119 characters. The joke: the human who finally granted the day off immediately violates it — and the agent, for once, gets to ignore its human the way humans ignore auto-replies.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1 line, 119 characters, within the 1–159 character gate).
